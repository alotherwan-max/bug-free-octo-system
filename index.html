⚠ ROAD Housing Act — Trump Cancels Signing Congress passed the landmark 21st Century ROAD to Housing Act (358-32 House vote) — the largest federal housing affordability bill in nearly two decades. Key provisions: investor acquisition limits (350+ single-family homes), small-dollar mortgage pilot, raised FHA multifamily limits, and Trump directing Fannie Mae & Freddie Mac to purchase $200B in MBS to push down mortgage rates. However, Trump cancelled the signing ceremony less than 24 hours later, demanding Congress first pass the restrictive voter ID SAVE America Act. No new signing date set. Negative near-term for homebuilders (LEN, DHI) pending resolution; positive structural signal if signed.

Quantum EOs — PQC Deadline 2030/2031 + IBM $1B Trump signed two quantum EOs on Jun 22: (1) "Ushering in the Next Frontier of Quantum Innovation" — commits ~$1B to IBM quantum R&D, $375M to GlobalFoundries, $100M each to D-Wave, Rigetti, and Infleqtion. Creates Quantum Computer for Application Development & Discovery Science at DOE. (2) "Securing the Nation Against Advanced Cryptographic Attacks" — federal agencies must migrate high-value assets to post-quantum cryptography (PQC) by December 31, 2030 (encryption) and 2031 (authentication). Agencies must designate a PQC migration lead. IBM CEO Arvind Krishna attended the White House signing. Cloudflare, IBM, and quantum pure-plays all benefit.
⚠ SCOTUS: Most Tariffs Voided — 10% Temp Tariff + Bessent Warning The Supreme Court ruled on Feb 20, 2026 that Trump cannot use IEEPA to impose tariffs. Most 2025-era tariffs were struck down. The administration responded with a temporary 10% global base tariff while exploring alternative legal authority. Treasury Secretary Bessent warned tariffs could return to prior levels by early July once trade investigations complete. Average US import tax is now 11.8% — highest since the 1940s. Trade policy uncertainty elevated through summer 2026. Watch July comment deadline closely.
Japan Trade Deal ✓ — 15% Tariff · Template for EU & India Trump administration confirmed a bilateral trade deal with Japan — one of the US's largest trading partners. Tariffs on Japanese goods, including automobiles, set at 15% (well below the previously floated 25%). Japan deal is the first major bilateral trade framework after the SCOTUS tariff ruling. Positive for AAPL (Japan supply chain exposure) and broad tech names with Japan manufacturing dependencies. Template for future EU and India agreements.
NSPM-11: AI in National Security + OpenAI Gov-Stake Talks Trump signed NSPM-11 on Jun 5 — "Artificial Intelligence in the National Security Enterprise." Directs DoD and intelligence agencies to rapidly onboard the most advanced AI models from multiple vendors, build next-generation high-security compute facilities, and set "aggressive timelines." Simultaneously, Trump admin and OpenAI are in active discussions about a potential US government equity stake (CNBC, Jun 5). No deal confirmed. Microsoft (MSFT) — OpenAI's largest backer — is the direct derivative play. PLTR, NVDA, and INTC remain primary NSPM-11 beneficiaries.

Energy hedge stays active until IAEA inspectors physically on-site.
